## 2. Roles, Access Boundaries & Server Redaction Map

Company roles seeded idempotently in [`app/Services/Tenancy/CompanyRoleService.php`](../app/Services/Tenancy/CompanyRoleService.php):

| Role / Actor | Primary Accessible Modules | Restricted / Protected Capabilities (Server-Redacted or Blocked) |
|---|---|---|
| `Owner` | Full company catalog, Settings, AI & Phase 9 governance | Company-scoped (no cross-tenant or platform routes unless granted platform perms). |
| `Administrator` | Company ops, Settings (`company`, `users`, `roles`, `sequences`, `taxes`, `money_accounts`, `purchases`), Audit, Payroll | Excluded by default from `PHASE9_PERMISSIONS` (`catalogs.*`, `settings.documents.manage`, `money.receipt.share`, `purchasing.document.pdf`, `inventory.barcode_labels.print`) & `AI_PERMISSIONS` unless Owner grants. |
| `Manager` | Ops + read-only `settings.company.view`/`users.view`/`audit.events.view` + sequence/tax/account/purchase settings + all `reports.*` + HR/Payroll | No `settings.company.manage`, `settings.roles.*`, `settings.users.manage`, `PHASE9_PERMISSIONS`, or `AI_PERMISSIONS`. |
| `Sales` | Invoices (draft/post), Quotations, Returns, Customers, `inventory.stock.view`, Receipts, Incoming checks, `reports.sales.view` | No `sales.invoice.change_price`, `sales.invoice.change_discount`, `inventory.cost.view`, `purchasing.*`, `reports.profit.view` / `reports.cost.view`. |
| `Purchasing` | Purchases (draft/post), Landed cost, Purchase returns, Vendors, `inventory.stock.view`, Vendor payments, Outgoing checks, `reports.purchases.view`, `money.expense.view` | No `sales.*`, `customers.*`, `money.cash.view` / `money.bank.view` / receipts, `inventory.cost.view` (unless granted). |
| `Warehouse` | Products (`inventory.product.manage`), Stock Overview, Adjustments, Transfers, Expiry, `reports.inventory.view` | No `inventory.cost.view`, `purchasing.cost.view`, sales, purchasing docs, or money accounts. |
| `Cashier` | Invoice create/view, Customers/statements/PDF, `inventory.stock.view`, `money.cash.view`, Receipts & Incoming checks | Cannot `sales.invoice.post`, `edit_draft`, `void`, `change_price`, `change_discount`, or view bank balances/reports. |
| `Viewer` | Read-only views (`sales.*`, `purchasing.purchase.view`, `inventory.stock.view`, `customers`, `vendors`, `money.*`, basic `sales`/`purchases`/`inventory` reports) | No writes; no `inventory.cost.view`, `purchasing.cost.view`, `reports.profit.view`, `reports.cost.view`, `reports.financial.view`, or `vendors.statement.view`. |
| `Platform Operator` | Platform routes (`platform:company.read`, `company.provision`, `subscription.manage`, `audit.read`, `company.impersonate`) | Session-bounded support impersonation; read browsing requires no repeated password prompt; provisioning/billing keep password confirmation; tenant PII shielded outside audited support view. |
