# Accounting

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Arabic-speaking small merchants and their authorized colleagues manage sales, purchases, inventory and accounts. English remains supported. The assistant helps answer permitted business questions and prepare editable invoices without replacing the manual workflow.

## Confirmed constraints

Laravel 13, Livewire 4 and the existing Tailwind design system. Preserve canonical reports and financial services, exact values, company isolation and protected permissions. AI never saves or posts a financial transaction. DeepSeek Flash is the sole built-in text provider for this product iteration. Browser speech is optional and requires truthful processing disclosure.

## Owner-approved journey

Enter a masked API key, explicitly approve the disclosed cost/privacy terms, confirm the account password, test the connection and start using the assistant. Advanced governance stays available separately. Authorized users find a persistent launcher and use a responsive Arabic RTL / English LTR chat sheet. This is an extension of the existing application identity, not an application-wide redesign.

## Authorities

The Owner's 12 October 2026 final product directive supersedes earlier multi-provider onboarding requirements. Accounting policy remains governed by the Master Specification, Engineering Blueprint and accepted ADRs. Protected originals remain untouched.
